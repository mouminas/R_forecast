\textbf{\textit{Understanding the impact of short-term climate risks on US crop insurance participation}}\\
$\bullet$ Studied the heterogeneity in the crop insurance response of different groups of farmers

past insurance type and crop choices, as well as their irrigation practice.\\
$\bullet$ Recommended that the Risk Management Agency create a premium differential between farmers who irrigate and those who do not in areas with low irrigation to prevent adverse selection in the crop insurance program as weather risk increases.\\[1.5mm]
\textbf{\textit{Mitigating climate risks and uncertainties through insurance vs. investment}}\\
$\bullet$ Created a general model, using Expected Utility (EU) and Robust Optimization (RO) theories, explaining the optimal choice of an economic producer
between direct investment and insurance to mitigate short-term vs. long-term
stochastics (risks and uncertainties).\\
$\bullet$
Recommended that insurance policymakers promote the combination of insurance coverage with self-protection measures, particularly in regions where producers have short-term perspectives.\\[2 mm]
\textbf{\textit{Understanding the greenium between green bonds and conventional bonds}}\\
$\bullet$ Developed a model to price green bonds as an equilibrium of their supply and demand.\\
$\bullet$ Recommended that policymakers strengthen environmental policy and invest in research to improve green technology efficiency, as it has a positive direct effect on
the equilibrium price of green bonds.
\end{position}
